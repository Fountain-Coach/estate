## One publication, one source

**`benedikt-eickhoff.de` and `fountain.coach` point to the exact same source-backed public Reframe Reader and Bookshelf.** These are two ways of entering **one site**, not two separately authored websites, multiple Bookshelves or alternative personal/company catalogues. Neither domain implies a separate book or reader experience.

The intended single public Reader route/tree and immutable publication artifact are authored in the **existing `Fountain-Coach/fountain.coach` source** and composed through Fountain Press from legitimately admitted works and editions. Reframe owns work/Bookshelf membership and writer authority; the original manuscripts, Governance normative chapters, Teatro/Satzfunk instruments, media and technical references remain owned by their genuine sources. The existing `Fountain-Coach/benedikt-eickhoff.de` Git source becomes retained source/provenance for historic personal publications and potential admitted materials, **not a second independent Reader site**.

Inside the same public reading source, **Benedikt Eickhoff** is the author/creator identity and **Fountain Coach** is the publisher/product identity. A small **Author / About** element belongs to the same publication and is not a personal-site pointer; there is no separate **Estate**, **Editorial** or **Works** website/brand inside the navigation. Actual works and source-faithful editions appear in the Bookshelf, with plain legal/technology/reference links in the footer.

## Domain behavior, not semantic duplication

Delivery can serve the same immutable bytes at both hosts or transparently redirect the name domain to a single canonical Reader origin. The practical redirect/alias choice is **an implementation and publication decision**: choose it based on deep-link continuity, canonical and Open Graph metadata, browser origin-specific preferences/bookmarks, independent TLS and existing DNS/email records. A shared work/edition/source identity does **not** prove that browser-local reading positions automatically synchronize across different origins. Use a single canonical edition URL and confirm any promised continuity.

The old personal site and its content, old deep links, other hosts, DNS/TLS/MX/email and source-owned assets remain protected until each genuinely required destination is verified and a separate authorized release is approved. No duplicate site build or parallel content-editing workflow is permissible as the intended final architecture. Existing technical `EstatePublisher` and stage `/estate/` are internal compatibility identifiers, not public labels.
