## Public language and reader arrangement

**Author's naming correction (29 October 2026): retire “Estate” completely as a public-facing term, not just one navigation item. Do not replace it with “Editorial”.** The word *editorial* properly describes the decisions and work of selecting, ordering, revising and preparing publications; as a noun an *editorial* is generally a publication's opinion article. It is not a name for the reader's destination or the user's relation to the works. The principal entrance is **Reframe Reader / Bookshelf**, with genuine book and edition titles, **Author / About** for a named person, and the **Fountain Coach** publisher identity. Instruments, MIDI2, reference and legal destinations are plain accessible links, ordinarily in the footer. There is no parallel top-level “Works” catalogue; individual works live in the Bookshelf. This explicitly supersedes the weaker earlier decision merely to demote the old label in navigation.

**Authoring and source acceptance:** new or revised static routes, masthead, header/reel, footer, breadcrumbs, metadata, accessible names, titles and social previews must not present “Estate” or “Editorial” as public branding, a site category or a destination. Preserve authored quotations using those words and all old public URLs, source repos, checksums and release receipts. Legacy technical IDs, including the installed `EstatePublisher` publishing contract and this repository's historical identity, are not public labels and cannot safely be renamed by a site-copy change.

## Source and publication arrangement

Fountain Coach's publications have accumulated websites that advertise **hostnames as if they were distinct destinations for readers**. The principal public entrance should instead become the **free Reframe Reader**, with Fountain Press Bookshelf state for source-authentic, actually admitted and released books. A book is identified by work/edition/source, not a hostname. A book's instrument is an admitted capability, not an additional book merely because it has a technical site.

**This is content redistribution, not simply fewer links.** Source-bearing material from any retiring public destination must be individually mapped into its legitimate new containing edition, authorial name-domain identity, publisher publication, or maintained plain reference route—with original source, owner, contributor credits, multilingual versions, digest and deep-link continuity. The author sees fewer domains because their existing contents have been semantically placed, not because material was hidden or deleted. Keep an unmatched original route live until it has an independently verified destination.

### Author / publisher rather than an infrastructure category

**Remove the old infrastructure term from the complete public reading vocabulary**, not the publishing machinery that serves the works. The name `benedikt-eickhoff.de` identifies the actual author/creator and may be the reader's small **Author / About** entry. `fountain.coach` remains the company/product and canonical free Reader entrance; they need not be two competing catalogues. Preserve truthful distinct legal/company and person/creator identities and all credited collaborators.

**Works ceases to be a primary destination** *after* its individual editions open from the Bookshelf. The original `works.benedikt-eickhoff.de` URLs (including its source-owned delegated `/solstice/` path), PDFs, bilingual text, credits, incoming links and archives remain independently reachable pending separately approved route redirects. Less front-door chrome comes **before** optional hostname retirement. This public decision does not touch domain DNS, TLS, MX/email, Caddy, backlinks or source Git owners.

### Plain, accessible supporting websites

The author proposes moving the current equally prominent editorial destinations `instruments.fountain.coach` (real capability catalogue), `midi2.fountain.coach` (technical specification/implementation/evidence), `book.fountain.coach` (reviewed Reframe reference and history), `satzfunk.fountain.coach` (authorized audio/citation), and `status.fountain.coach` (transparency) to restrained **Technology**, **Reference** and **Company / Legal** footer groups. These remain completely functional *direct-static, compliant public websites*, each under its original source/rights owner. They do not need an imagined book cover or fake Linotype page to remain available to readers. Footers must preserve accessible links, imprint/privacy/accessibility and original normative references.

The final reel/footer geometry and any true domain retirement require a separately reviewed staged reader. The existing nine-entry reel and previous three-publication catalogue are preserved as historical/live state until the new route is accepted and deployed.

2. Compose the free **Bookshelf and first books** through the existing Fountain Press reading contract on owner Git, preserving all real URLs and independent source/asset paths.
3. Give **Governance** an immutable *complete* edition and explicit chapter-status/canonical-current relation before claiming it as an admitted public Linotype book.
4. Reduce the top navigation to book/edition reading and an appropriate author/publisher link; remove all visible “Estate” and “Editorial” public destination wording and place technical, legal and instrument references in a coherent **accessible** footer, checking every old-to-new route and metadata/accessible-name projection.
5. Stage only through the registered owner-WLAN EstatePublisher/SSH contract, retaining source/published production baseline and predecessor. Require real browser/AX and Safari/iPad acceptance for the layout.
6. A separate rights-approved immutable public release and independent HTTPS verification must succeed **before** retiring old entrances or configuring redirects. Retain exact rollback and no source/credits loss.

**This is not a new universal template runtime, CMS, authority service, or shortcut to website retirement.** The canonical current Governance website, original Works repositories, public MIDI2 evidence, instrument API/catalogue and Status/legal services remain authoritative within their actual scopes.
