# Reframe the Estate — books, author, instruments and reference

**29 October 2026 · editor/author-directed sorting proposal.**  
**State:** Proposed editorial destination and migration path; **not** a deployed route, changed server, admitted FountainStore book, new rights grant, DNS removal or published release.  
**Owning product direction:** private Reframe `scenarios/027-fewer-domains-bookshelf-editions.md`, inheriting free Reader / paid Writer ADR-007. This public document describes only the publication-facing plan.

## Editorial sorting

The Fountain Coach publication Estate has accumulated websites that advertise **hostnames as if they were editorial categories**. The principal public entrance should instead become the **free Reframe Reader**, with Fountain Press Bookshelf state for source-authentic, actually admitted and released books. A book is identified by work/edition/source, not a hostname. A book's instrument is an admitted capability, not an additional book merely because it has a technical site.

**This is content redistribution, not simply fewer links.** Source-bearing material from any retiring public destination must be individually mapped into its legitimate new containing edition, authorial name-domain identity, publisher publication, or maintained plain reference route—with original source, owner, contributor credits, multilingual versions, digest and deep-link continuity. The author sees fewer domains because their existing contents have been semantically placed, not because material was hidden or deleted. Keep an unmatched original route live until it has an independently verified destination.

- **Essays — A Book Continually Written:** one ongoing, versioned book, with editorially admitted reading sequences such as latest-first; individual complete essays and permanent addresses survive.
- **First Reading / Solstice:** retain the exact original edited edition and original source owner, present it as a book instead of an external Works-host destination.

Original documents, facsimile limits, credits and original direct URLs survive. A source-owner proposal is not an automatic public Reframe book.
- **Governance — complete numbered edition:** the author now intends the entire current normative publication to be a source-faithful **Governance book edition**, with future amendments becoming **new explicit editions**. The current 29 October source reports 160 numbered chapters; this is a snapshot count, not a fixed invented chapter limit. The canonical `governance.fountain.coach` source continues to own chapter authority and direct links. Original chapter IDs, texts, images, status labels, exact digests and citations must remain intact. A selected-constitutional anthology, abridgment or AI rewrite is not the complete Governance book.
- **Teatro — An Unfinished Composition:** its authentic authored essay/first-hearing is a book; the separately owned Teatro/Csound stage and hearing/score instruments may be invoked from the reader when genuinely admitted. No false narrated recording.

Other individual works or dailies join only after authentic work/edition/cover/rights and author-admitted membership. The technical inventory is not a shelf count.

### Author / publisher rather than another Estate cover

**Remove “Estate” as a primary navigation destination**, not the operating system that publishes the works. The name `benedikt-eickhoff.de` identifies the actual author/creator and may be the reader's small editorial/about/publisher entry. `fountain.coach` remains the company/product and canonical free Reader entrance; they need not be two competing catalogues. Preserve truthful distinct legal/company and person/creator identities and all credited collaborators.

**Works ceases to be a primary destination** *after* its individual editions open from the Bookshelf. The original `works.benedikt-eickhoff.de` URLs (including its source-owned delegated `/solstice/` path), PDFs, bilingual text, credits, incoming links and archives remain independently reachable pending separately approved route redirects. Less front-door chrome comes **before** optional hostname retirement. This public decision does not touch domain DNS, TLS, MX/email, Caddy, backlinks or source Git owners.

### Plain, accessible supporting websites

The author proposes moving the current equally prominent editorial destinations `instruments.fountain.coach` (real capability catalogue), `midi2.fountain.coach` (technical specification/implementation/evidence), `book.fountain.coach` (reviewed Reframe reference and history), `satzfunk.fountain.coach` (authorized audio/citation), and `status.fountain.coach` (transparency) to restrained **Technology**, **Reference** and **Company / Legal** footer groups. These remain completely functional *direct-static, compliant public websites*, each under its original source/rights owner. They do not need an imagined book cover or fake Linotype page to remain part of the Estate. Footers must preserve accessible links, imprint/privacy/accessibility and original normative references.

The final reel/footer geometry and any true domain retirement require a separately reviewed staged reader. The existing nine-entry reel and previous three-publication catalogue are preserved as historical/live state until the new route is accepted and deployed.

## Migration order and evidence

1. Establish **source-true identity, rights and state** of each selected work/edition and exact owner Git SHA. Work ≠ edition ≠ book admission ≠ public release; unknown ≠ no.
2. Compose the free **Bookshelf and first books** through the existing Fountain Press reading contract on owner Git, preserving all real URLs and independent source/asset paths.
3. Give **Governance** an immutable *complete* edition and explicit chapter-status/canonical-current relation before claiming it as an admitted public Linotype book.
4. Reduce the top navigation, promote author identity and place technical, legal and instrument references in a coherent **accessible** footer, checking every old-to-new route.
5. Stage only through the registered owner-WLAN EstatePublisher/SSH contract, retaining source/published production baseline and predecessor. Require real browser/AX and Safari/iPad acceptance for the layout.
6. A separate rights-approved immutable public release and independent HTTPS verification must succeed **before** retiring old entrances or configuring redirects. Retain exact rollback and no source/credits loss.

**This is not a new universal template runtime, CMS, authority service, or shortcut to website retirement.** The canonical current Governance website, original Works repositories, public MIDI2 evidence, instrument API/catalogue and Status/legal services remain authoritative within their actual scopes.
