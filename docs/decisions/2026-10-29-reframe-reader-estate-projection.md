**Decision date:** 29 October 2026

## Publication-facing decision

**Later author naming correction (29 October 2026):** “Estate” is dropped completely from public-facing site identity, navigation, metadata, masthead and accessibility names; do not substitute “Editorial” as a destination. The proper names are **Reframe Reader / Bookshelf**, **Author / About**, individual book/edition titles and **Fountain Coach** as publisher. The original repository/domain inventories, source Git IDs, old deep links and internal `EstatePublisher` operation names remain valid compatibility details, not public branding. Apply this correction to future authoring and stage candidates; it is not itself a completed deployment. [See the publication naming decision](2026-10-29-bookshelf-estate-reframing.md).

The principal Fountain Coach public experience on both canonical WLAN staging and authorized public production should be the **free Reframe Reader**. Its primary view is a real Fountain Press **Bookshelf/Reader** of authorized released, source-bound editions, not the paid Reframe Writer, a promotional site-directory substitute or an interface that only resembles a book.

The **paid Reframe Writer** adds the capability to create and edit authorized authored source, scores, media and semantic relationships in a versioned, permissioned workflow. **Publication is a separate author/editorial rights and EstatePublisher operation.** Paid access alone never grants the right to edit other authors' works, incur provider costs, release private material or publish an edition.

**Interaction is available to readers where published.** Readers may use author-approved chapter orders, semantic links, listening/scrubbing interfaces, authorized Teatro stages and admitted performance instruments without changing the authored edition. A personal reading position is distinct from a source revision. Missing rights or unavailable capabilities must be disclosed, not emulated or taken as permission to run a private service.

## Books and instruments

Books are admitted attributed source-bound works/editions with exact source identity, revision, approved edition relationship, authentic cover and genuine Linotype reader. A web route, domain, PDF candidate, static page, score, audio track, Empty shelf cell, MIDI2 instrument or executable stage is **not** automatically a book.

An instrument is a **capability that a book can offer**, subject to actual admission, provenance, author approval, runtime and Reader permissions. The source body remains in the Linotype page frame; sound, timelines, scores and executable scenes are composed to fit its reading semantics. Fountain Press alone composes the book. Domain owners retain their individual content and rights. The existing Fountain Coach Publication Core governs complete checked-in direct-static routes; it is not replaced by a new global live renderer.

The public Reader must preserve truthful distinctions among admitted independent volumes, the continuing Essays book, source-complete versioned Governance as the proposed Bookshelf edition, MIDI2/reference materials, instrument catalogue, legal/footer surfaces, existing public routes and the complete owner/asset inventory. A cover grid is a **reader entrance**, not an inventory of all owned domains.

## Environments and bounded next acceptance

This records the direction **without** activating or modifying staging or production. Source development stays in owner Git repositories. The single canonical private staging remains the registered Ubuntu Mac mini; production stays the actual public HTTPS state and separately governed publication receipts. A Reader release candidate must preserve original complete route bytes and rights alongside a genuine Bookshelf/Press reading journey, with real allowed instrument behavior and independently witnessed browser/accessibility evidence. An iPad-size screenshot alone does not establish Safari or accessibility compliance.

This decision supplies no public source authorization, private media release, DNS change, Writer/paywall implementation, publication approval or license. The next implementation work is a **bounded Reader scenario**; the Writer entitlement and Editor/Composer acceptance are independent work, and the final public promotion is another explicit act.
